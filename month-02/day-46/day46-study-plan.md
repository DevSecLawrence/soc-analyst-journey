## The Approach
 
30 minutes of Security+ study daily, running parallel to the roadmap. Not replacing hands-on work — adding to it. The roadmap continues. Security+ study happens on top.
 
Why parallel and not sequential: stopping the roadmap to study Security+ means 60+ days of no new hands-on work to show on GitHub. Recruiters who check the commit history will see a gap. Keeping both going means the portfolio keeps growing while the cert knowledge builds.
 
The risk: doing both at once means neither gets 100% attention. The answer to that is prioritisation — Security+ study covers my weakest domains first so even partial preparation closes the biggest gaps.

## Week by Week Plan
 
### Weeks 1–2 (Days 46–60) — Domain 5 First
 
**Why Domain 5 first:** It's 20% of the exam and I scored ~10% ready. Every hour here has the highest return.
 
- Governance elements — policies, standards, procedures, regulations
- Risk management — ALE, SLE, ARO calculations (these will be on the exam)
- Compliance frameworks — GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS, NIST — what each covers and who it applies to
- Data classification and privacy concepts
**Daily:** 1 Professor Messer video (Domain 5) + 10 ExamCompass questions on that topic
